Our Journey

Born from Love.

Aura was founded on a simple principle: that the first years of a child's life should be surrounded by the purest materials and the most thoughtful design.

What started as a personal quest for the softest organic cotton has grown into a global community of parents who refuse to compromise on quality or aesthetics. We believe that your home should feel like a sanctuary, and every piece of baby gear should compliment that harmony.

"We don't just sell baby products; we curate the environment in which your child's first memories are made."

— Elena Ross, Founder

Our Foundations

Values that guide us.

100% Organic

We exclusively use GOTS-certified organic cotton and sustainable materials that are gentle on both your baby and the planet.

Ethically Sourced

Every artisan in our supply chain is treated with respect, ensuring fair wages and safe working conditions across the globe.

Heirloom Quality

Our pieces are designed with timeless aesthetics and durable construction, meant to be cherished and passed down.
