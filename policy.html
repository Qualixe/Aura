Privacy Policy

How we handle your data

Last updated: January 1, 2026

At Aura Baby, your privacy is fundamental to everything we do. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and password when you create an account.
  • Order information: billing address, shipping address, and payment details (processed securely — we never store full card numbers).
  • Usage data: pages visited, products viewed, and interactions with our site to improve your experience.
  • Communications: messages you send us via email or our contact form.

How We Use Your Information

We use your data to process and fulfil orders, send order confirmations and shipping updates, personalise your shopping experience, and improve our products and services. We will only send marketing emails if you have opted in, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

We never sell your data. Your personal information is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Data Retention & Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, email us at privacy@aurababy.com. We retain order data for 7 years as required by law, but all other personal data is deleted upon request.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep your cart and session active, and optional analytics cookies to understand how visitors use our site. See our Cookie Policy for full details.

Shipping & Returns

Delivery & Return Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

Shipping Options

  • Standard Shipping (US): 3–5 business days — Free on orders over $75, otherwise $6.95.
  • Express Shipping (US): 1–2 business days — $14.95.
  • International: 7–14 business days — Free on orders over $150, otherwise calculated at checkout.
Orders placed before 2pm EST Monday–Friday are processed the same day. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed the next business day.

Returns

We want you to love every Aura purchase. If you're not completely satisfied, we accept returns within 30 days of delivery for unused items in their original packaging.

How to Return

  • Email hello@aurababy.com with your order number and reason for return.
  • We'll send you a prepaid return label within 1 business day.
  • Pack the item securely and drop it off at any authorised carrier location.
  • Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days of receiving your return.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Sale and clearance items (final sale).
  • Personalised or custom-made products.
  • Items that have been used, washed, or are not in original condition.

Sustainability

Our Commitment to the Planet

Last updated: January 1, 2026

Sustainability isn't a marketing term for us — it's a core operating principle. We believe that caring for your child and caring for the planet are inseparable.

Materials

  • 100% GOTS-certified organic cotton across all textile products.
  • FSC-certified sustainable wood for all wooden toys and furniture.
  • OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certified — tested for 100+ harmful substances.
  • Non-toxic, water-based dyes and food-grade finishes on all wooden items.

Packaging

Since June 2022, Aura Baby has been 100% plastic-free in all packaging. We use recycled kraft paper, natural twine, and soy-based inks. Our mailer boxes are made from 80% post-consumer recycled content and are fully recyclable.

Carbon Footprint

We offset 100% of our shipping emissions through verified carbon offset programmes. We are working towards becoming a certified B Corporation by 2027 and have committed to net-zero operations by 2030.

1% for the Planet: We donate 1% of all revenue to environmental non-profits focused on reforestation and ocean conservation.

Artisan Partnerships

Every artisan in our supply chain is paid a living wage and works in safe, verified conditions. We conduct annual audits of all manufacturing partners and publish the results in our annual transparency report.

Cookie Policy

How we use cookies

Last updated: January 1, 2026

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. We use them to make the site work properly and to understand how it's being used.

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be switched off. They are set in response to actions you take, such as adding items to your cart or logging in to your account.

Analytics Cookies

With your consent, we use analytics cookies (via a privacy-first analytics provider) to understand which pages are most popular and how visitors navigate the site. This data is aggregated and anonymous — it cannot be used to identify you.

Marketing Cookies

We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies. We do not share your browsing behaviour with ad networks.

Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of the site. For more information, visit allaboutcookies.org.

Questions about our cookie use? Email us at privacy@aurababy.com and we'll respond within 2 business days.
