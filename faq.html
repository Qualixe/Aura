Help Centre

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our products, orders, and policies. Can't find an answer? Reach out to us.

Orders & Shipping

How long does standard shipping take?
Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days within the US. Express shipping (1–2 business days) is available at checkout. International orders typically arrive within 7–14 business days depending on destination.
Do you offer free shipping?
Yes — all US orders over $75 qualify for free standard shipping. For international orders, free shipping is available on orders over $150. The discount is applied automatically at checkout.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we ship to over 50 countries worldwide. Shipping costs and estimated delivery times are calculated at checkout based on your location. Please note that import duties and taxes may apply and are the responsibility of the recipient.
How do I track my order?
Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation email with a tracking number. You can use this number on our carrier's website or visit your account dashboard to follow your package in real time.
Can I change or cancel my order?
Orders can be modified or cancelled within 1 hour of placement. After that, our fulfilment team begins processing and changes may not be possible. Please contact us immediately at hello@aurababy.com if you need to make a change.

Products

What materials do you use?
We exclusively use GOTS-certified organic cotton, sustainable beechwood, non-toxic water-based dyes, and hypoallergenic materials. Every material is rigorously tested to ensure it is free from harmful chemicals and safe for newborn skin.
Are your products safe for newborns?
Absolutely. All Aura products are designed with newborn safety as the top priority. Our textiles are OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certified, meaning they have been tested for over 100 harmful substances. Our wooden toys use only food-grade finishes.
How do I care for my Aura products?
Most of our textiles can be machine washed on a gentle cold cycle and tumble dried on low. Wooden items should be wiped clean with a damp cloth and dried immediately. Specific care instructions are included on each product's label and detail page.
Do you offer gift wrapping?
Yes! Gift wrapping is available for all orders. You can select this option at checkout and include a personalised message. Our packaging uses recycled kraft paper and natural twine — beautiful and planet-friendly.

Returns & Refunds

What is your return policy?
We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for unused items in their original packaging. Sale items and personalised products are final sale. To initiate a return, visit your account dashboard or email us at hello@aurababy.com.
How long do refunds take?
Once we receive and inspect your return, refunds are processed within 3–5 business days. The credit will appear on your original payment method within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.
What if my item arrives damaged?
We're so sorry if this happens. Please photograph the damage and email us at hello@aurababy.com within 48 hours of delivery. We will arrange a replacement or full refund at no cost to you — no need to return the damaged item.

Account & Payments

What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal. All transactions are secured with SSL encryption and processed through our PCI-compliant payment gateway.
Is my payment information secure?
Yes. We never store your full card details on our servers. All payment data is handled by our PCI DSS Level 1 certified payment processor. Your information is always encrypted and protected.
Do you offer a loyalty programme?
Yes — the Aura Circle rewards programme lets you earn points on every purchase, which can be redeemed for discounts on future orders. You also get early access to new collections and exclusive member-only offers. Sign up is free.

Still have questions?

Our team is available Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm EST.
