Common Questions

What materials do you use?
We strictly use GOTS-certified organic cotton, sustainable beechwood, non-toxic water-based dyes, and hypoallergenic materials to ensure absolute safety for your child.
What is your return policy?
We accept returns within 30 days of purchase for unused items in their original packaging. Simply initiate a return through our portal.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we offer worldwide shipping. Shipping costs and transit times are calculated at checkout based on your location.
